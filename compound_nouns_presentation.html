What Is a Compound Noun?

A compound noun is a noun made from two or more words. Together, the words name one person, place, thing, or idea.

A tooth + a brush = a toothbrush
A rain + a bow = a rainbow

The meaning of a compound noun is connected to its parts, but sometimes the whole word has a special meaning.

The spelling pattern can vary. Learn the usual spelling of each compound noun.

1. Closed Compound Nouns

These compound nouns are written as one word, without a space or hyphen.

⌨️

Keyboard

key + board

🧁

Cupcake

cup + cake

🍉

Watermelon

water + melon

🛹

Skateboard

skate + board

☃️

Snowman

snow + man

🧑‍🚒

Firefighter

fire + fighter

2. Open Compound Nouns

These compound nouns are written as two separate words.

🏠

Living room

living + room

🚌

School bus

school + bus

🪥

Hair brush

hair + brush

🏊

Swimming pool

swimming + pool

🛝

Play area

play + area

🧺

Laundry basket

laundry + basket

3. Hyphenated Compound Nouns

These compound nouns use a hyphen (-) between their parts.

🥈

🚶

🛎️

🎯

👨‍👩‍👦

Compound Nouns in Sentences

1. My little brother ate a chocolate cupcake.
2. I use my keyboard to type words.
3. We made a snowman in winter.
4. The school bus arrived at eight o'clock.
5. Dad put the clothes in the laundry basket.

Compound nouns can be the subject or the object in a sentence.
